Support

How can we help?

Pepperlily Studio designs and develops apps in-house — from concept to App Store. If you have a question about one of our products, you're in the right place.

Who makes your apps?
All apps published by Pepperlily Studio are designed and developed entirely in-house. We handle everything from UX research and product design to development and release.
How do I report a bug or issue?
Please email us with a description of the issue, your device model, and OS version. We typically respond within 1–2 business days.
How do I request a feature?
We love hearing from users. Send us your ideas at the email below and we'll consider them for future updates.
Where can I find your privacy policy?
Each app includes a privacy policy link within the app and on its App Store listing page.
